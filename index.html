03 / INFORMATION

Rules & guidelines.

Information for players submitting a level or record.

Rules are not finalized

These are starter guidelines. List staff should publish the exact minimum speed, allowed increments, and submission process before accepting records.

01

Speed requirements

Submissions must be completed above the original level's intended speed. The minimum multiplier and permitted increments are to be determined by list staff. Include the exact multiplier with every submission.

02

Verification

The verifier is the first player to complete the submitted version at its listed speed under the list's rules. A verifier's completion counts toward their leaderboard points.

03

MegaHack & timewarp

Disclose the tools and settings used in your run, including MegaHack and any timewarp or speed-modifying features. List staff must define which versions, settings, and modifications are permitted.

04

Raw footage

Provide unedited raw footage of the complete run, including the level and speed settings, along with a publicly accessible video. Additional evidence may be requested by list staff.
