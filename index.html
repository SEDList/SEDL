03 / THE GUIDELINES

RULES & GUIDELINES.

Everything you need to know before submitting a run.

!
Community guidelines template

These are starter guidelines, not an approved ruleset. List administrators should publish their exact minimum speed, allowed increments, and submission process before accepting records.

01 / SPEED

Turn up the pace.

Submissions must be completed at a speed above the original level's intended speed. The minimum multiplier and permitted speed increments are to be determined by list staff. Include the exact multiplier with every submission.

02 / VERIFICATION

Earn the verification.

The verifier is the first player to complete the submitted version at its listed speed under the list's rules. A verifier's completion counts toward their leaderboard points.

03 / TOOLS

Keep it transparent.

Disclose the tools and settings used in your run, including MegaHack and any timewarp or speed-modifying features. List staff must define which versions, settings, and modifications are permitted before reviewing submissions.

04 / EVIDENCE

Show the full run.

Provide unedited raw footage of the complete run, including the level and speed settings, along with a publicly accessible video. Additional evidence may be requested by list staff.
